Mailchimp vs Kit

Email marketing for businesses and creators

MarketingLast checked 21 November 2026

Overview

Mailchimp and Kit (formerly ConvertKit) are both email marketing platforms with free plans, signup forms, automations and pricing that grows with your list. Mailchimp is built for small businesses and ecommerce brands, with SMS and ads alongside email. Kit is built for creators, with landing pages, paid newsletters and digital products in the same account.

The biggest differences are how far the free plan goes, how sends are limited, where automations start, and how many people can use the account. This page sets them side by side using each company's own pricing pages and help docs.

Which should you choose?

MChoose Mailchimp if…

  • You run a small business or online store and want email, SMS and ads in one place.
  • Your list is small and you want a low-cost paid plan: Essentials starts at $13 a month for 500 contacts.
  • You have a larger team: Premium includes unlimited seats and role-based access.
  • You want SMS marketing alongside email, as an add-on.
About Mailchimp

KChoose Kit if…

  • You're a creator and want a free plan for up to 10,000 subscribers.
  • You want to sell paid newsletters, digital products or courses from the same account.
  • You'd rather pay by subscriber count without a monthly send limit on your plan.
  • You want unlimited landing pages and opt-in forms, even on the free plan.

Key differences

  • Free plan

    Kit's free Newsletter plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers, with one basic automation, one sequence and one user. Mailchimp's Free plan covers up to 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with a daily limit of 250.

  • Entry price

    Mailchimp Essentials starts at $13 a month for 500 contacts. Kit Creator starts at $39 a month for up to 1,000 subscribers, or $33 a month billed yearly. Both rise as your list grows.

  • Send limits

    Mailchimp plans include a monthly send limit that rises with the plan and contact count. Kit charges by subscriber count, so sending more emails doesn't raise the price.

  • Automations

    Kit includes one basic automation on the free plan and unlimited Visual Automations and sequences on paid plans. Mailchimp's multi-step customer journeys start on Standard, from $20 a month.

  • Earning from your list

    Kit lets creators sell paid newsletter subscriptions, digital products and courses on every plan, with transaction fees. Mailchimp's platform centres on marketing channels: email, SMS as an add-on, social posts and ads, and landing pages.

  • Teams

    Mailchimp Essentials includes 3 seats and Premium has unlimited seats with role-based access. Kit includes 1 user on the free plan, 2 on Creator and unlimited users on Pro.

What they have in common

  • Free plans to get started
  • Pricing that rises with list size
  • Landing pages and signup forms
  • Automated emails and customer journeys
  • A 14-day free trial on paid plans
  • A/B testing and reporting on paid plans

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Mailchimp or Kit?

For a small list, Mailchimp's paid plans start lower: Essentials is $13 a month for 500 contacts, while Kit Creator is $39 a month for up to 1,000 subscribers ($33 billed yearly). For a free plan, Kit goes much further: up to 10,000 subscribers, compared with 250 contacts on Mailchimp Free.

Is Kit the same as ConvertKit?

Yes. Kit is the new name of ConvertKit, the email marketing platform for creators.

Can I sell products or paid newsletters?

Kit lets you sell paid newsletter subscriptions, digital products and courses on every plan, including Free, with transaction fees. Mailchimp's plans centre on email, SMS, social and ads marketing, and its pricing pages don't list paid newsletters.

Do both have a free trial?

Yes. Both offer a 14-day free trial on paid plans. Kit's trial doesn't need a credit card; on Mailchimp's Standard trial, you add a payment method to unlock the full send allowance.
