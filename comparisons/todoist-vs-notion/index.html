Todoist vs Notion

Task management: focus vs flexibility

ProductivityLast checked 28 November 2026

Overview

Todoist and Notion both help you manage tasks, but they come at the problem from opposite directions. Todoist, launched in 2007 by bootstrapped company Doist, is a focused task manager built for speed — you capture, organise and complete tasks with minimal friction. Notion, founded in 2016, is a connected workspace that combines docs, databases, wikis and project management in one flexible tool.

Both offer free plans. Todoist Pro starts at $5 per month billed yearly. Notion Plus starts at $10 per seat per month billed yearly. The core trade-off: Todoist does one thing — task management — and does it fast; Notion does many things and lets you build your own system, but that flexibility takes more setup.

Which should you choose?

TChoose Todoist if…

  • You want fast, distraction-free task capture with natural language input and smart scheduling.
  • You need a simple, reliable to-do list that works the same on every platform — web, desktop, mobile, watch and email.
  • You prefer a tool that works out of the box with no setup or configuration required.
  • You are an individual or small team that wants personal task management at a low price.
About Todoist

NChoose Notion if…

  • You need a workspace that combines tasks, docs, wikis, databases and project views in one tool.
  • You want to build custom workflows with linked databases, relations, rollups and formulas.
  • You are a team that needs shared documentation and project management in a single platform.
  • You want built-in AI for writing assistance, summaries and Q&A across your workspace.

Key differences

  • Focus and complexity

    Todoist is a task manager. You open it, type a task, and it is captured. Natural language processing picks up dates, priorities and labels automatically. Notion is a workspace platform where tasks are one of many things you can build. You can create powerful custom project systems, but they require setup and configuration. If you want to manage tasks with minimal friction, Todoist wins. If you want tasks integrated into a broader workspace, Notion is the platform.

  • Task capture speed

    Todoist is built for speed. Quick Add lets you type a task with natural language like 'Review proposal tomorrow at 2pm p1 #Work' and Todoist parses the date, priority and label automatically. It works from every platform — including email forwarding, browser extensions and Apple Watch. Notion's task creation is functional but requires more clicks and does not have the same natural language parsing.

  • Project views and flexibility

    Notion offers six project views — list, board, calendar, gallery, timeline and table — with linked databases, relations, rollups and formulas for building custom workflows. Todoist offers three views — list, board and calendar — with filters and labels for organising tasks. For teams with complex, multi-dimensional projects, Notion's database model is far more powerful. For straightforward task lists, Todoist's simplicity is an advantage.

  • Documentation and knowledge

    Notion is a full docs and wiki platform. You can write documentation, build team wikis, create meeting notes and link everything to your projects and tasks. Todoist is strictly a task manager — it has task descriptions and comments, but no document or wiki capabilities. If your team needs docs and tasks in one place, Notion is the only choice.

  • AI features

    Notion AI is deeply integrated: it can write and edit text, summarise pages, answer questions about your workspace, autofill database properties and generate content. Todoist's AI Assistant helps with task suggestions and smart scheduling but is narrower in scope. For AI-assisted productivity across docs and projects, Notion's AI is more capable.

  • Pricing and value

    Todoist Pro at $5 per month (yearly) is one of the most affordable productivity tools available. Notion Plus at $10 per seat per month (yearly) costs twice as much but includes docs, wikis, databases and project management — features you would need separate tools for otherwise. For pure task management, Todoist is the better value. For an all-in-one workspace, Notion can replace multiple tools and may save money overall.

What they have in common

  • Free plans with core functionality
  • Cross-platform apps for web, desktop and mobile
  • Recurring tasks and reminders
  • Team collaboration with shared projects
  • Integrations with Slack, Google Calendar and other tools
  • API access for custom integrations

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for personal task management?

Todoist. It is faster to capture tasks, simpler to use and available on more platforms including Apple Watch. Its natural language input and smart scheduling make personal task management effortless. Notion can do it too, but requires more setup.

Which is better for teams?

Notion, in most cases. Teams typically need shared documentation, project views and databases alongside task management. Notion combines all of these in one workspace. Todoist works well for teams that only need a shared task list, especially at its lower price point.

Can I use both together?

Yes, and many people do. A common setup is Todoist for personal daily tasks and quick capture, with Notion as the team workspace for docs, projects and knowledge management. They can be connected through integrations.

Which has the better free plan?

Notion's free plan is more generous: unlimited pages, blocks and guest collaborators. Todoist's free plan limits you to 5 personal projects with basic features. For getting started without paying, Notion offers more.
