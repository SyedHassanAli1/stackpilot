ChatGPT vs Claude

AI assistants for writing, research & coding

AI ToolsLast checked 21 November 2026

Overview

ChatGPT, made by OpenAI, and Claude, made by Anthropic, are two of the most widely used AI assistants. Both help with writing, research, analysis and coding, both can search the web and work with files, and both run on the web, Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android.

The differences are in the details: how the plans are priced and billed, how usage is limited, which coding tool is included, and whether ads may appear. This page sets them side by side using each company's own pricing and help pages.

Which should you choose?

CChoose ChatGPT if…

  • You want a low-cost paid plan: Go is $8 a month.
  • You want image creation built into the same app.
  • You want unlimited everyday text chats, even on the free plan.
  • You want to try agentic coding for free: Codex is included, with limits, on the free plan.
About ChatGPT

✳Choose Claude if…

  • You want an assistant that stays ad-free on every plan, including Free.
  • You prefer to pay yearly for a discount: Pro is $17 a month billed yearly.
  • You want connectors, memory and Artifacts on the free plan.
  • You write code and want Claude Code, included from the Pro plan.

Key differences

  • Plan ladder

    ChatGPT has more steps between free and the top tier: Go at $8, Plus at $20 and Pro from $100 to $500 a month. Claude goes from Free to Pro ($17 billed yearly or $20 monthly), then Max at $100 and Ultra at $200.

  • Billing

    ChatGPT's Go, Plus and Pro plans are billed monthly only. Claude Pro can be paid yearly ($200 up front) for a lower monthly price; Max and Ultra are monthly only.

  • Usage limits

    ChatGPT includes unlimited everyday text chats on every plan, subject to guardrails, and limits tools and advanced models by plan. Claude limits usage per 5-hour window and per week on every plan, with higher limits on higher plans.

  • Coding

    ChatGPT includes Codex, with limited use on the free plan and more on paid plans. Claude Code is included from the Pro plan and isn't available on Free.

  • Images

    ChatGPT can create images on every plan, with limits on Free. Claude's plan pages don't list image creation.

  • Ads

    OpenAI has said ads may appear on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans, while Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise stay ad-free. Anthropic has said Claude will remain ad-free.

  • Enterprise pricing

    ChatGPT Enterprise pricing isn't published. Claude Enterprise lists $20 per seat a month plus usage at API rates, with a 20-seat minimum.

What they have in common

  • Free plans with web search
  • Memory that carries context across conversations
  • Projects to organise chats and files
  • Apps for web, Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android
  • A $20/month standard plan when billed monthly
  • Team plans at $20 per user a month billed yearly, or $25 monthly

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT or Claude cheaper?

Both have free plans. ChatGPT's cheapest paid plan, Go, costs $8 a month. Claude's cheapest paid plan, Pro, costs $17 a month billed yearly or $20 monthly, the same as ChatGPT Plus when paid monthly. Their team plans cost the same: $20 per user a month billed yearly, or $25 monthly.

Which one includes a coding tool?

Both. ChatGPT includes Codex, with limited use on the free plan. Claude includes Claude Code from the Pro plan; it isn't part of the free plan.

Do ChatGPT or Claude show ads?

OpenAI has said ads may appear on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans; Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise stay ad-free. Anthropic has said Claude will remain ad-free.

Can I pay yearly?

ChatGPT's Go, Plus and Pro plans are billed monthly only; Business can be billed yearly. Claude Pro can be billed yearly for $200, and Team can be billed yearly; Max and Ultra are monthly only.
