Webflow

Visual website development with CMS and hosting.

DesignWebsite BuildersFreemium
Visit Webflow (opens official site in a new tab) Framer vs Webflow

Overview

Webflow is a visual website platform for designers, marketers and developers. Its Designer maps directly to real HTML and CSS, so you build layouts with classes, flexbox and grid visually, then publish to Webflow's managed hosting without writing code.

A structured CMS, GSAP-powered interactions, SEO controls and form handling are built in, and Webflow AI and an MCP server let AI agents build and edit sites. Pricing has two parts: a Site plan for each published site, and a Workspace plan for your team's seats and features. Add-on products such as Localize, Analyze and Optimize are priced separately.

Key features

  • Visual Designer

    Build responsive layouts visually with classes, flexbox and grid that map to real HTML and CSS.

  • Webflow CMS

    Structured content collections for blogs, directories and resources, available from the Premium plan.

  • Interactions with GSAP

    Design animations visually, including scroll triggers, text splitting and timelines, powered by GSAP.

  • Webflow AI and MCP

    AI credits in every Workspace, plus an MCP server that lets AI agents build pages, CMS content and interactions.

  • Hosting and forms

    Managed hosting with custom domains on paid Site plans, form handling and password protection.

  • Code components and Cloud

    Bring in code components on Premium, and host full apps with Webflow Cloud.

Best for

  • Marketing and company websites
  • CMS-driven blogs, resource hubs and directories
  • Animated, interaction-rich landing pages
  • Agency and freelance client sites
  • Sites that marketing teams update without developers

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Fine-grained visual control over real HTML and CSS layout.
  • A structured CMS with up to 20,000 items on the Premium plan.
  • Built-in animations powered by GSAP, with presets you can reuse.
  • Hosting, CMS, forms and SEO controls in one platform.

Limitations

  • The free Starter plan is limited to a webflow.io domain, 2 static pages and 50 form submissions.
  • The Basic plan has no CMS; you need Premium for CMS collections.
  • Costs have two parts: a Site plan per site, plus Workspace plans and seats for your team.
  • Localize, Analyze and Optimize are separate paid add-ons.
  • Its CSS-based Designer suits people comfortable with web layout concepts.

Pricing

Free Starter plan. Each published site needs a Site plan, and teams also pay for a Workspace plan and seats. Prices below are billed yearly; monthly billing costs more (Basic $25/mo, Premium $39/mo).
