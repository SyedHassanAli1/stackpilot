ChatGPT

Conversational AI assistant for everyday tasks.

AI ToolsAI AssistantsFreemium
Visit ChatGPT (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI assistant. It helps with writing, brainstorming, research, analysis and coding, and can search the web, work with uploaded files, create images and hold voice conversations.

Its desktop app combines Chat, ChatGPT Work and Codex in one place, so you can run longer projects, work with files and code across your computer. Every plan includes unlimited everyday text chats, subject to guardrails; paid plans add advanced reasoning models, more uploads, image creation, deep research and Codex usage. ChatGPT runs on the web, Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android.

Key features

  • Chat and web search

    Unlimited everyday text chats on every plan, with real-time answers from the web.

  • Files, images and voice

    Upload files, create images and talk to ChatGPT by voice, with higher limits on paid plans.

  • Deep research

    Run multi-step research on a topic; limited on Free and Go, expanded on Plus and Pro.

  • Codex and ChatGPT Work

    Agentic coding and long-running work in the desktop app, sharing one usage allowance.

  • Projects and memory

    Organise chats and files into projects, with memory that grows on higher plans.

  • Custom GPTs and tasks

    Use custom GPTs and schedule tasks to run later; scheduled tasks start on the Go plan.

Best for

  • Writing, editing and brainstorming
  • Research and analysis with web search
  • Software development with Codex
  • Creating images for posts and presentations
  • Team work with shared projects on Business

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • A free plan with unlimited everyday text chats, web search and projects.
  • A wide range of tools in one app: research, images, voice and coding.
  • Desktop apps for Windows and Mac, plus iPhone and Android apps.
  • Plans scale from an $8/mo Go plan up to Pro, Business and Enterprise.

Limitations

  • Uploads, image creation, voice, deep research and memory are limited on the free plan.
  • The Go plan may include ads.
  • Advanced reasoning models need Plus or higher, and usage limits apply.
  • Pro starts at $100/mo, and its fastest mode needs the $500/mo tier.
  • Business needs at least 2 users; Enterprise and Edu pricing isn't published.

Pricing

Free plan available. Prices are in USD; Go pricing is localized in some markets. Business and Enterprise teams can buy credits for more access, and nonprofits can get up to 75% off Business or Enterprise.
