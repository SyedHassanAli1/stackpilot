Google Gemini

Google’s AI assistant for chat, research, writing and images, connected to Google apps.

AI ToolsAI AssistantsFreemium
Visit Google Gemini (opens official site in a new tab) Claude vs Gemini

Overview

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. It helps with chat, research, writing and coding, creates and edits images, and can hold spoken conversations with Gemini Live. It's available on the web, in Android and iOS apps, and in desktop apps for Windows and macOS.

Gemini connects with Google services: on paid plans it works inside Gmail, Docs and other Google apps, and it's built into Chrome. Google AI plans bundle higher Gemini usage with extra Google One storage and other benefits, such as video generation, Google Flow credits and Google Antigravity for coding. Business users can get Gemini through Google Workspace or Gemini Enterprise.

Key features

  • Chat and Deep Research

    Everyday help in chat, plus Deep Research for in-depth reports, available on the free plan.

  • Images and video

    Generate and edit images for free; video generation comes with Google AI plans.

  • Gemini Live and Canvas

    Talk to Gemini by voice, and draft documents and code together in Canvas.

  • Gemini in Google apps

    Use Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids and Chrome on paid plans.

  • Gemini Notebook

    A research and writing assistant for your sources, with Audio Overviews.

  • Google Antigravity

    Build apps and prototype ideas with a coding agent, built into the Gemini macOS app.

Best for

  • Everyday questions, writing and brainstorming
  • Research reports with Deep Research
  • Working in Gmail, Docs and other Google apps
  • Creating images and short videos
  • Coding and prototyping with Google Antigravity

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • A capable free plan with Deep Research, image generation, Gemini Live and Canvas.
  • Works inside Gmail, Docs, Chrome and other Google apps on paid plans.
  • Plans include Google One storage, from 400 GB on Plus to 5 TB on Pro.
  • Available on the web, Android, iOS, Windows and macOS.

Limitations

  • The free plan uses a lighter model and has the lowest usage limits.
  • Gemini in Gmail, Docs and other Google apps needs a paid plan.
  • Ultra costs $99.99/mo or $199.99/mo for the highest limits.
  • Prices and some features vary by country.
  • Work and school accounts need an admin to enable Gemini, or a Workspace plan.
