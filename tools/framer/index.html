Framer

Design and publish interactive, responsive websites.

DesignWebsite BuildersFreemium
Visit Framer (opens official site in a new tab) Framer vs Webflow

Overview

Framer is a no-code website builder for designers and marketing teams. You design pages on a free-form visual canvas, with components, breakpoints and styles, and publish them as fast, server-rendered websites without handing the work to an engineer.

Hosting, a CMS, SEO controls, analytics and localization are built in, and AI agents can generate, edit and translate pages while keeping everything editable on the canvas. Framer is built for websites: it isn't a design file tool for app interfaces, and it doesn't generate application code. Plans are priced per site, with a free plan for publishing on a Framer domain.

Key features

  • Visual canvas

    Design responsive pages with components, breakpoints and styles, then publish directly from the canvas.

  • AI agents

    Generate, edit and maintain pages with AI. Every plan includes monthly AI credits.

  • Built-in CMS

    Collections for articles, careers, events or directories, with conditional visibility based on content.

  • Localization

    Translate pages and CMS content manually or with AI, with locale-specific slugs and redirects.

  • SEO and performance

    Server-rendered pages with metadata, sitemaps and structured data; redirects on the Pro plan.

  • Hosting and analytics

    Managed hosting and built-in analytics, plus staging and branch previews on the Pro plan.

Best for

  • Marketing sites and landing pages
  • Portfolios and personal websites
  • Startup and product websites
  • CMS-driven blogs, directories and careers pages
  • Multilingual websites

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Design and publish from one visual canvas, without a developer handoff.
  • Hosting, CMS, SEO controls and analytics are included in one platform.
  • Built-in localization, with AI translation that keeps locales in sync as you edit.
  • A free plan lets you build and publish a site on a Framer domain.

Limitations

  • Free sites use a Framer domain and show a “Made in Framer” banner; a custom domain needs a paid plan.
  • Each site needs its own plan, and extra editors cost $20/mo each (the workspace owner is free).
  • The Basic plan includes only 2 CMS collections; Pro raises this to 10.
  • Some features are paid add-ons, including localization on Basic, A/B testing and advanced hosting.
  • Built for websites — not for app interface design files or generating application code.

Pricing

Free plan; paid plans are priced per site. The workspace owner is free, extra editors cost $20/mo and Content Editors $10/mo, and viewers are free.
