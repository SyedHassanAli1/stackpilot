Sentry

Application monitoring for errors, performance, replays and logs.

DevelopmentMonitoringFreemium
Visit Sentry (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Sentry is an application monitoring platform that captures errors, performance traces, session replays and logs from your apps, then groups them so developers can find and fix problems faster. It supports most major languages and frameworks.

Pricing is event-based: each plan includes a base volume per data type, such as errors, replays and logs, and you can add more with discounted reserved volume or a pay-as-you-go budget. The Developer plan is free forever, and the Business plan has a 14-day free trial with no credit card.

Key features

  • Error monitoring

    Capture errors with stack traces and context, grouped into issues.

  • Tracing

    Follow requests across services to find slow code and bottlenecks.

  • Session Replay

    Watch replays of user sessions to see what led to an error.

  • Logs and metrics

    5 GB of logs and 5 GB of application metrics included on every plan.

  • Flexible volume

    Add reserved volume at a discount, or set a pay-as-you-go budget.

  • Many SDKs

    SDKs for most major languages and frameworks, web and mobile.

Best for

  • Catching production errors
  • Debugging performance problems
  • Seeing what users did before a crash
  • Monitoring web and mobile apps
  • Engineering teams triaging issues

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • A free Developer plan, forever.
  • Errors, tracing, replays and logs in one place.
  • Pay only for the data you send, with reserved volume discounts.
  • A 14-day Business trial with unlimited event data.

Limitations

  • The Developer plan is limited in features and volume.
  • Event-based pricing needs monitoring as your traffic grows.
  • Extra logs and metrics beyond 5 GB cost $0.50/GB.
  • Advanced features need Business, at $80/mo or more.
  • Usage beyond plan quotas needs a pay-as-you-go budget.
