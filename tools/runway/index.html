Runway

Generative AI studio for creating and editing video and images.

AI ToolsVideo GenerationFreemium
Visit Runway (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Runway is a generative AI platform for creating and editing video and images. Its own models include Gen-4.5 for text-to-video and image-to-video, and Aleph 2.0 for editing existing videos with text prompts, alongside third-party models such as Kling, Seedance and Nano Banana Pro in the same workspace.

Usage is measured in credits: each model costs a set number of credits per second of video or per image. The free plan includes a one-time deposit of 125 credits to explore, and paid plans include a monthly allotment. Developers can use the Runway Dev API, which is billed separately with pay-as-you-go credits.

Key features

  • Gen-4.5 video

    Text-to-video and image-to-video generation, 2 to 10 seconds per clip.

  • Aleph 2.0 editing

    Edit existing videos with text prompts and keyframe images.

  • Image generation

    Create images with Runway and third-party image models.

  • Third-party models

    Kling, Seedance, Nano Banana Pro and more, with full access from Pro.

  • Upscaling and voice

    4K video upscaling, plus Text to Speech and Custom Voices from Pro.

  • Team workspaces

    Shared workspaces with pooled credits, commenting and free viewer seats on Team.

Best for

  • Short AI video clips for social and ads
  • Editing and restyling existing footage
  • Concept videos and storyboards
  • Images for creative projects
  • Adding AI video to apps via the API

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Several leading video and image models in one workspace.
  • Video editing as well as generation, with Aleph 2.0.
  • A free plan with credits that don't expire.
  • Yearly billing lowers the price of every paid plan.

Limitations

  • The free plan has only 125 one-time credits and a limited set of tools.
  • Credits can't be bought on the free plan.
  • Video uses credits quickly; Standard's 625 credits cover about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5.
  • Access to all models and 4K upscaling needs Pro or higher.
  • Standard, Pro and Max are single-user; inviting members needs Team.
