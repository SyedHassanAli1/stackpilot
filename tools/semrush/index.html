Semrush

Online visibility platform for SEO, content and ads research.

MarketingSEOFreemium
Visit Semrush (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Semrush is a marketing platform for SEO, content marketing, competitor research and paid search. It covers keyword research, site audits, rank tracking, backlinks and competitor analysis, plus separate toolkits for local SEO, content, traffic analysis and PR.

Semrush sells its tools as toolkits. Semrush One bundles the SEO Toolkit with the AI Visibility Toolkit, to track your brand in both Google and AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini, and is the recommended starting point for most teams. The classic SEO Toolkit is still available in Pro, Guru and Business tiers, and a free account gives limited daily access.

Key features

  • Keyword research

    Find keywords with volume, difficulty and intent data, and compare competitors with Keyword Gap.

  • Site Audit

    Crawl your site to find technical SEO issues.

  • Position Tracking

    Track keyword rankings across search engines and AI tools.

  • Competitor research

    See competitors' organic rankings, traffic and backlinks.

  • AI Visibility Toolkit

    Track how your brand appears in AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini.

  • Content tools

    SEO Writing Assistant and Topic Research on Guru and higher.

Best for

  • Keyword research and SEO planning
  • Competitor and market research
  • Rank tracking across search and AI
  • Technical site audits
  • Agencies managing multiple client sites

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Covers SEO, content, competitor research and ads in one platform.
  • Tracks visibility in both Google and AI tools.
  • A free account with limited daily searches.
  • Yearly billing lowers the monthly cost.

Limitations

  • The free account is limited to 10 requests a day.
  • SEO Toolkit plans start at $139.95/mo, billed monthly.
  • Many tools are separate toolkits with their own prices.
  • Historical data and content tools need Guru or higher.
  • Extra users cost $45 to $100 each per month, depending on the tier.

Pricing

Free account available. Prices below are billed monthly; yearly billing is cheaper (for example, SEO Toolkit Pro is $117.33/mo yearly). Other toolkits, such as Local, Content and Traffic & Market, are priced separately.
