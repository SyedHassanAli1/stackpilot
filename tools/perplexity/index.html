Perplexity

AI-powered answer engine with cited sources.

AI ToolsAI Search & ResearchFreemium
Visit Perplexity (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Perplexity is an AI answer engine. It researches the open web in real time and returns concise answers with in-line citations, so you can check where each claim comes from and ask follow-up questions.

Behind the scenes it routes each question to the model best suited for it. Beyond search, Deep Research writes in-depth reports and can create presentations, spreadsheets and dashboards, Comet is Perplexity's AI-powered browser, and Computer runs multi-step tasks. Free users get core search and chat; paid plans unlock Pro Search, advanced models, file uploads, image generation and higher limits.

Key features

  • Cited answers

    Real-time web search with concise answers and in-line citations for every response.

  • Deep Research

    In-depth reports with more sources and charts, plus presentations, spreadsheets and dashboards.

  • Comet browser

    An AI browser that summarises pages, extracts information and completes tasks on the web.

  • Computer

    An assistant for multi-step tasks that researches, analyses, creates and codes from start to finish.

  • Spaces and files

    Organise research in Spaces and ask questions about uploaded files and documents.

  • Model choice

    Perplexity picks a model for you, and paid plans can use advanced models from several providers.

Best for

  • Quick, cited answers to research questions
  • In-depth reports and market research
  • Comparing sources, documents and data
  • Browsing and web tasks with the Comet browser
  • Team knowledge search on Enterprise plans

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Answers include in-line citations, so you can check sources.
  • Practically unlimited basic searches on the free plan.
  • Research and creation in one place, from reports to spreadsheets and dashboards.
  • Available on the web, in iOS and Android apps, and through the Comet browser.

Limitations

  • The free plan has very limited Pro Searches and no advanced models or image generation.
  • Max costs $200/mo for the highest access to advanced models and file and app creation.
  • Enterprise is billed per seat: $40/mo for Enterprise Pro and $325/mo for Enterprise Max.
  • Computer usage on paid plans is limited by credits that reset over time.
  • Answers are only as reliable as the sources it finds, so citations are worth checking.
