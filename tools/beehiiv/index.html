beehiiv

Newsletter platform with website, growth tools and monetisation.

MarketingEmail & NewslettersFreemium
Visit beehiiv (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

beehiiv is a newsletter platform built for growth. It combines newsletters, a website and podcasts in one place, with unlimited email sends, campaign analytics, custom domains, an AI website builder and a recommendation network to grow your audience.

Paid plans add ways to earn, such as the ad network, paid subscriptions and boosts, plus referral programs, automations and advanced analytics. Prices rise with your subscriber count. The free Launch plan supports up to 2,500 subscribers, and Enterprise covers lists of 100,000 or more.

Key features

  • Newsletters and website

    Publish newsletters, a website and podcasts from one platform.

  • Unlimited sends

    Send as many emails as you like, with optimised deliverability, on every plan.

  • Growth tools

    A recommendation network on Free, plus referral programs and boosts on paid plans.

  • Monetisation

    Earn with the ad network and paid subscriptions, from the Scale plan.

  • Automations and analytics

    Automations, A/B testing and advanced analytics on paid plans.

  • API and MCP

    API access and beehiiv MCP, with read access on the free plan.

Best for

  • Launching a newsletter
  • Growing a newsletter audience
  • Earning from ads and paid subscriptions
  • Running a newsletter website
  • Media brands with several publications

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • A free plan for up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends.
  • Newsletter, website and podcast in one platform.
  • Built-in monetisation through ads and paid subscriptions.
  • A recommendation network to grow your list.

Limitations

  • The free plan is capped at 2,500 subscribers.
  • Monetisation, automations and advanced analytics need Scale or higher.
  • Prices rise with your subscriber count.
  • Removing beehiiv branding needs the Max plan.
  • Lists over 100,000 subscribers need Enterprise.

Pricing
