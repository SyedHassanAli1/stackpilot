Buffer

Schedule social posts, reply to comments and track performance.

MarketingSocial MediaFreemium
Visit Buffer (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Buffer is a social media management tool for planning and scheduling posts, engaging with your audience and tracking performance across channels. It connects to 11 platforms, including X, Bluesky, Mastodon and Google Business, with a queue and a visual content calendar.

Buffer is priced per connected channel rather than per user, with volume discounts as you add more. The free plan supports up to three channels with 10 scheduled posts each, plus an AI Assistant and API and MCP access. Essentials adds unlimited posts and deeper analytics, and Team adds unlimited users and approval workflows.

Key features

  • Scheduling and queue

    Plan posts in a queue and a visual content calendar.

  • Ideas and drafts

    Save ideas and drafts, with unlimited ideas on paid plans.

  • Community replies

    Reply to comments, with AI reply suggestions and saved replies.

  • Analytics

    Track performance, with deeper analytics on paid plans.

  • AI Assistant

    Generate and improve post ideas with AI, on every plan.

  • Approvals and teams

    Approval workflows and unlimited team members on the Team plan.

Best for

  • Scheduling posts for creators
  • Small business social media
  • Managing several brands' channels
  • Team approval workflows
  • Agencies managing client accounts

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • A free plan for up to 3 channels.
  • Priced per channel, not per user.
  • Team includes unlimited users at no extra cost.
  • Volume discounts as you add more channels.

Limitations

  • The free plan allows only 10 scheduled posts per channel.
  • Essentials is for one user; collaboration needs Team.
  • Costs rise with the number of channels you connect.
  • AI replies are limited to 5 suggestions a week on Free.
  • Approval workflows need the Team plan.

Pricing

Free plan for up to 3 channels. Paid plans are priced per channel, with lower rates for more channels; prices below are for the first 10 channels, billed monthly, and yearly billing is cheaper.
