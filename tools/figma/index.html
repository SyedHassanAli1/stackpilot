Figma

Collaborative interface design and prototyping in the browser.

DesignUI/UX DesignFreemium
Visit Figma (opens official site in a new tab) Canva vs Figma

Overview

Figma is a collaborative design platform that runs in the browser, with desktop apps also available. Teams use it to design interfaces for websites and apps, build interactive prototypes and maintain shared component libraries, and several people can work in the same file at once.

A Figma seat covers more than the core design tool. Dev Mode handles developer handoff, FigJam is a whiteboard for workshops, Figma Slides is for presentations, Figma Make turns prompts into working prototypes and Figma Draw adds illustration tools. Newer products such as Figma Sites and Figma Buzz are in beta. The free Starter plan suits individuals and small experiments; paid plans add unlimited team files, Dev Mode and shared libraries.

Key features

  • Real-time collaboration

    Several people can edit, comment and present in the same file at once, directly in the browser.

  • Interactive prototyping

    Link frames into clickable flows with transitions to test ideas and share them with reviewers.

  • Components and libraries

    Turn any group of layers into a reusable component and share libraries across a team on paid plans.

  • Auto layout

    Frames that resize automatically with their content, for responsive interface design.

  • Dev Mode

    Developers inspect designs, see what changed, read annotations and connect tools like Jira, Storybook and GitHub. Paid plans only.

  • Figma Make and AI

    Turn prompts into working code prototypes. Every seat includes a monthly allowance of AI credits.

Best for

  • Designing web and mobile app interfaces
  • Building and maintaining a design system
  • Clickable prototypes for user testing and reviews
  • Design-to-development handoff
  • Workshops and brainstorming in FigJam

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Runs in the browser, so reviewers and collaborators need nothing installed.
  • Free Starter plan with unlimited drafts that can be shared with unlimited viewers.
  • One Full seat covers design, whiteboarding, presentations, prototyping and handoff.
  • Can co-edit with unlimited people, even on the free plan's team files.

Limitations

  • The free plan's team space is limited to 3 design files and 3 FigJam files, with 3 pages per file.
  • Dev Mode is not available on the free Starter plan.
  • Priced per seat, with Full, Dev and Collab seats charged separately, so costs grow with team size.
  • Cloud-based only: files are stored by Figma, with no self-hosted option.
  • Some newer products, such as Figma Sites and Figma Buzz, are still in beta.

Pricing

Free Starter plan; paid plans are priced per seat. Professional prices shown are billed yearly; a Full seat is $20/mo billed monthly. View seats are free on every plan.
