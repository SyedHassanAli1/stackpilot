Plausible Analytics

Lightweight, privacy-friendly web analytics without cookies.

MarketingWeb AnalyticsPaid
Visit Plausible Analytics (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Plausible is a lightweight, privacy-friendly web analytics tool and an alternative to Google Analytics. It shows all key metrics on one simple dashboard, such as top pages, referral sources and goals, without using cookies, so you don't need a cookie consent banner for it.

Plausible is a self-funded company that charges a subscription and doesn't sell data or run an ad business. Plans are priced by monthly pageviews rather than seats, with a 30-day free trial and no free plan. The open-source Community Edition can be self-hosted for free, though a few features are only on the cloud version.

Key features

  • Simple dashboard

    All key metrics on one page: visitors, top pages, sources, locations and devices.

  • No cookies

    Privacy-friendly tracking without cookies, so no consent banner is needed for it.

  • Goals and custom events

    Track conversions and custom events, with saved segments and annotations.

  • Google Analytics import

    Import your historical data from Google Analytics.

  • Funnels and revenue

    Funnels, user journeys and revenue tracking on the Business plan.

  • Stats API and sharing

    Share dashboards with clients, embed them, and pull data with the Stats API on Business.

Best for

  • Privacy-friendly analytics for websites
  • Replacing Google Analytics with something simpler
  • Agencies sharing dashboards with clients
  • Blogs and content sites
  • Teams that want to avoid cookie banners

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Privacy-friendly, with no cookies.
  • A simple dashboard that's easy for clients and teams to read.
  • Priced by pageviews, not seats; yearly billing gives 2 months free.
  • Open source, with a free self-hosted Community Edition.

Limitations

  • There's no free cloud plan, only a 30-day trial.
  • Starter covers one site only; Growth allows up to 3 sites and team members.
  • Funnels, revenue tracking and the Stats API need the Business plan.
  • Some features, such as funnels and SSO, aren't in the self-hosted edition.

Pricing

30-day free trial, no credit card required. Prices below are for up to 10,000 monthly pageviews, billed monthly, and rise with traffic. Yearly billing gives 2 months free. Business plans get 15% off for nonprofits, education and open-source projects on yearly billing.
