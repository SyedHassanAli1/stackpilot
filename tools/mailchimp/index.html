Mailchimp

Email marketing, automation and audience management.

MarketingEmail & NewslettersFreemium
Visit Mailchimp (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Mailchimp is an email marketing platform for building campaigns, managing audiences, setting up automated customer journeys and tracking performance. It includes templates, signup forms and popups, A/B testing and reporting, with SMS available as an add-on.

Pricing is based on the number of contacts you store, so the price rises as your list grows. The free plan suits beginners with up to 250 contacts. Paid plans add more sends, automation and optimisation tools, and Premium adds unlimited seats and role-based access for teams.

Key features

  • Email campaigns

    Design and send campaigns with templates and a drag-and-drop editor.

  • Automations

    Set up automated customer journeys, with more advanced tools on Standard and up.

  • Signup forms

    Grow your list with embedded forms and popups.

  • Audience management

    Segment and tag contacts, with advanced segmentation on Premium.

  • Testing and analytics

    A/B testing on paid plans, multivariate testing on Premium, plus reporting.

  • SMS add-on

    Send SMS marketing alongside email, priced separately.

Best for

  • Newsletters for small businesses
  • Ecommerce marketing emails
  • Automated welcome and follow-up emails
  • Growing an email list with signup forms
  • Marketing teams sending at high volume

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • A free plan for getting started with up to 250 contacts.
  • Templates, forms, automations and reporting in one tool.
  • A 14-day free trial on paid plans.
  • Premium includes unlimited seats and role-based access.

Limitations

  • The free plan is limited to 250 contacts and 500 sends a month.
  • Pricing is based on contacts, so costs rise as your list grows.
  • SMS is a separate add-on, not included in the base price.
  • Advanced segmentation and multivariate testing need Premium, from $350/mo.
  • Phone support is only on Premium.
