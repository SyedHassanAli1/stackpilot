Google Analytics

Google’s free web and app analytics for traffic, behaviour and conversions.

MarketingWeb AnalyticsFree
Visit Google Analytics (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Google Analytics measures how people find and use your websites and apps. It uses event-based tracking, with reports on acquisition, engagement and conversions, real-time reporting, and machine learning that surfaces insights and fills gaps in data you can observe.

The standard version is free and integrates with Google's advertising and publisher tools. Large enterprises can upgrade to Analytics 360, a paid version with higher limits for data collection, reporting, retention and BigQuery export, guaranteed data freshness and service-level agreements.

Key features

  • Event-based tracking

    Measure user interactions across websites and apps with events.

  • Real-time reporting

    Monitor activity on your site or app as it happens.

  • Acquisition and engagement

    See where users come from and how they engage with your content.

  • Conversions and attribution

    Track key actions, with data-driven attribution modelling.

  • Machine learning insights

    Proactive insights and privacy-safe modelling for a fuller picture of the customer journey.

  • Google integrations

    Connect to Google Ads and other Google tools, plus BigQuery export.

Best for

  • Website traffic analysis
  • Measuring marketing campaigns
  • Conversion and funnel tracking
  • App analytics
  • Linking analytics with Google Ads

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Free for businesses of every size.
  • Deep integration with Google Ads and other Google tools.
  • Covers both websites and apps.
  • Analytics 360 is available for large enterprises with SLAs.

Limitations

  • Data freshness isn't guaranteed on the free version.
  • The free version has lower limits for custom dimensions, metrics and audiences.
  • Funnel reporting is more basic than in Analytics 360.
  • Analytics 360 pricing isn't published; it's based on a base fee plus event volume.

Pricing

The standard version of Google Analytics is free. Analytics 360 is a paid contract with a fixed base fee that includes the first 25 million monthly events, plus a per-event fee above that.
