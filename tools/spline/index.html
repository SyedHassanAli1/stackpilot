Spline

Browser-based 3D design tool for interactive scenes on the web and in apps.

Design3D & MotionFreemium
Visit Spline (opens official site in a new tab)

Overview

Spline is a 3D design tool that runs in your browser. You model with primitives, booleans and sculpting, add layered materials and lights, and bring scenes to life with states, keyframes, events and physics, then publish them to the web, iOS and Android.

Teams model, comment and review together in real time, and AI agents such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor can work in the same file through Spline MCP. The free plan includes the editor, the Spline Library and web exports with a watermark; paid plans add AI credits, unlimited files and more export options.

Key features

  • 3D modelling

    Model with primitives, booleans and sculpting, plus 3D generation with AI.

  • Materials and lighting

    Layered materials, lights and post-processing for real-time scenes.

  • Animation and interaction

    States, keyframes, events and physics for interactive 3D experiences.

  • Real-time collaboration

    Model, comment and review together in live files, with unlimited viewers.

  • Spline MCP

    AI agents like Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor can work in your files over MCP.

  • Web and app exports

    Publish to the web, with Apple and Android exports on the Pro plan.

Best for

  • Interactive 3D on websites
  • 3D UI elements and product visuals
  • Landing pages with 3D scenes
  • 3D assets for iOS and Android apps
  • Design teams prototyping in 3D

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Runs in the browser, with no install needed.
  • A free plan with the full editor and unlimited viewers.
  • Real-time collaboration, including with AI agents.
  • Yearly billing gives 2 months free.

Limitations

  • Free web exports include a Spline watermark, and personal files are limited.
  • Apple and Android exports, video export and APIs need the Pro plan.
  • Code and self-hosted exports need Enterprise.
  • SAML SSO and version history are only on Enterprise.
  • AI features use credits, with extra credits sold as an add-on.

Pricing

Free plan available. Paid plans are priced per seat and include monthly AI credits; yearly billing gives 2 months free. Every plan includes unlimited viewers.
